# Day 0: choosing

**Decided:** build lastlight, a sealed, camera-scored forecast of Phoenix twilight colour.

**Rejected alternatives:** a passive radar graded by ADS-B truth (closest runner-up: the best truth signal of any candidate, but prior art is strong, export-control questions are open for public radar code, and its scoreboard converges within two months); predicting downstream library breaks; home GNSS atmospheric scorecards; a Colorado River operations model. Reasons in [`../selection.md`](../selection.md).

**Why this over the radar:** a physical forecast of something everyone has an opinion about, judged by an instrument, with a new picture every evening even when the score is flat. The radar would have been the better engineering showcase and the worse thing to watch.

**Design changes forced by the red team:**

- Clouds are in scope from the start, because a clear-sky-only model paints near-identical strips all winter.
- Everything public is referenced to sun elevation, never clock time.
- A $0 gate (G0) must show that the rendered curve varies enough across a year of real atmospheres to be measurable before any hardware is bought.

**What broke:** nothing yet. The repo was created with GitHub's default branch name and renamed to `main`.

**Next:** scaffold, CI, privacy scanner, solar geometry, seal ledger, HRRR ingest.
